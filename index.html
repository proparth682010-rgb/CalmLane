    Designing for the days you don’t feel like it.

    Problem

    Exam stress, scattered tasks and endless feeds. Many productivity apps add pressure with red badges, streak shaming and dense dashboards, which are hardest to use on the days you need them most.

    Design hypothesis

    On low-energy days, people need fewer choices, not more features. This is a design hypothesis drawn from common student pain points; we haven’t run a formal study yet.

    Solution

    The Shapeshifter. One honest question reshapes colour, motion, density, copy and the recommended tool. Drained gets one tiny task. Energised gets the hard thing first.

    What we’d measure

    Sessions started on low-mood days, time to understand the product, and retention without streaks. These are goals for a pilot, not results.
